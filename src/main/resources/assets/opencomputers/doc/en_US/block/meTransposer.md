# ME Transposer

![Such a poser, but connected.](oredict:oc:meTransposer)

An upgraded [transposer](transposer.md) that additionally connects to an attached ME network, exposing it as the virtual side "me". This lets a [computer](../general/computer.md) move items to and from ME network storage the same way it would move them between two adjacent inventories with a regular transposer.

*Note that this block has no internal inventory, and requires Applied Energistics 2 to be installed.*
