# ME Dual Actuator

![Now with 100% more everything, and connected.](oredict:oc:meDualActuator)

A [ME Dual Transposer](meDualTransposer.md) with an [adapter](adapter.md) fused into it. It moves both items and fluids to and from an attached ME network through its virtual "me" side, it can interface with whatever block sits on its side the same way an adapter would, exposing it as a component to a connected [computer](../general/computer.md).

*Note that this block has no internal inventory, and requires Applied Energistics 2 to be installed.*
