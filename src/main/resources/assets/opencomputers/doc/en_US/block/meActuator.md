# ME Actuator

![Now with 100% more everything, and connected.](oredict:oc:meActuator)

A [ME Transposer](meTransposer.md) with an [adapter](adapter.md) fused into it. In addition to moving items to and from an attached ME network through its virtual "me" side, it interfaces with whatever block sits on its remaining side the same way an adapter would, exposing it as a component to a connected [computer](../general/computer.md).

*Note that this block has no internal inventory, and requires Applied Energistics 2 to be installed.*
