# ME Dual Transposer

![Such a poser, but connected.](oredict:oc:meDualTransposer)

An upgraded [transposer](transposer.md) that connects to an attached ME network on both sides of transferral: like the [ME Transposer](meTransposer.md), items can be moved to and from ME network storage through the virtual side "me", and this variant extends the same capability to fluids.

*Note that this block has no internal inventory, and requires Applied Energistics 2 to be installed.*
